# Code of Conduct

ShootingCSP is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free
research and software community.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and focus criticism on technical issues;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- respect differing scientific viewpoints;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disclosure of
  private information.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates these expectations.
